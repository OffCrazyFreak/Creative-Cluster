# Owner guide

This guide is for people who list themselves or their organization in this directory. Need help? Write to {{contactEmail}}.

## Apply for a listing

1. Open "Apply". You do not need an account.
2. While you type the name, we show similar listings. If yours is there, choose "Is this you? Claim it" instead of applying again.
3. Fill in the name, a short description, one way to contact you, and your country and town.
4. Tick the consent box and send the form.
5. Open the email we send you and click the link to confirm your address.
6. We review applications within 7 days. When yours is approved, we email you a link to set your password.

If we reject it, the email tells you why. You can edit the application and send it again. Rejected applications are deleted after 90 days.

## Verify your email

Click the link in the email we sent. The link only works for a limited time. If it has expired, apply again or write to us.

## Claim an imported listing

We added some listings from earlier lists. If yours is one of them, we email you a claim link.

1. Click the link in the email. It works for 30 days.
2. Check the details and set your password, or use Google.
3. Fix anything that is out of date.

If you do not want the listing, use the removal link in the same email. We will remove it. You will get one email and one reminder, nothing more. Listings nobody claims are archived after 12 months. You can ask us to restore one later.

## Set a password or use Google

- A password needs 12 or more characters. Spaces are fine. You can paste it. There are no other rules.
- Tip: use a sentence only you know, like "blue kettle sings on tuesday mornings". It is long and easy to remember.
- We check passwords against known leaked lists. If yours is on one, choose another.
- The strength bar is only advice. It never blocks you.
- To use Google, sign in with the same email address as your listing. Google sign-in does not create new accounts.

Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password" on the login page.

## Edit your listing

1. Sign in and open "My listing" (account, listing).
2. Change what you need and save.
3. Your changes go live at once. We log every change. Staff can undo a change that breaks the rules, and we email you if that happens. In rare cases we may ask to review your changes first. The page tells you when that applies.

## Descriptions and rich text

The description, references and opportunity text have a small editor. You can use bold, italic, underline, two heading levels, bullet and numbered lists, quotes and links. Links must start with http or https. If you paste from Word, the editor cleans the formatting. Names and short fields are plain text.

## Tags

- Choose up to 5 tags in each field: topic, what you offer, and what you seek.
- Cannot find a tag? Suggest one. You can use it on your listing at once. Others see it after an admin approves it.
- Tags power the "Could collaborate with" cards. If you do not want to appear there, turn matching off in your listing settings.

## Address and pin

1. Choose "Physical location" or "Online only". Online-only listings have no pin.
2. Start typing the address. After 3 characters we suggest places. Pick one.
3. Cannot find it? Choose "Can't find it? Enter manually" and give the town and country.
4. A small map shows a pin. Drag it to the right spot.
5. No mouse? Click the pin, then use the arrow keys. One press moves it 10 metres. Hold Shift for 100 metres. You can also type latitude and longitude, or press "Reset".
6. Once you move the pin by hand, we never move it back.

Saving is never blocked by a missing address.

## Who sees which field

Each contact field has a visibility setting:

- Public: anyone, including search engines.
- Members: only signed-in members of this directory.
- Hidden: only you and the admins.

If you set nothing, a field is shown to members only. Check the settings before you save.

## Logo and cover image

1. Open the image area in "My listing" and choose a file. JPEG, PNG or WebP only. SVG is not accepted.
2. A window lets you crop. The logo is square (1:1). The cover is wide (16:9).
3. Add a short description of the image for people who cannot see it. The default is your name.

We resize the logo to 256 pixels and the cover to 1200 pixels wide. Pictures are limited to 1 MB after resizing, and we do this in your browser. We remove camera data such as location from the file.

## Translation

We translate your text into the other language automatically after you save. It can take a few minutes.

- Visitors see "Machine translated from [language]" and a link to your original.
- You can read and edit the translation in your listing. When you edit it, it counts as your own text and we never overwrite it.
- If a translation fails our checks, we do not show it. Visitors see your original text with a notice.
- Check names, places and technical terms. Machines get them wrong.

## Opportunities

Opportunities are short posts such as "we seek a partner" or "we offer a workshop space". You must have a published listing to post.

- Up to 3 active posts at a time, and 3 new posts a day.
- Title up to 120 characters, text up to 1,000.
- Your first post is reviewed by an admin. Later posts go live at once.
- If you set a deadline, the post ends the day after it. Without a deadline it runs 60 days.
- To renew, open the post in the last 8 days or after it ends and choose "Renew". You can renew 3 times. No review is needed.
- Found what you needed? Choose "Mark as filled". The post shows "Filled" for 7 days and then closes.
- "My opportunities" shows which posts expire soon. We do not send reminder emails.
- Visitors answer through the "I can help" form. Your email stays private.

## Connections

Connections show who you work with.

1. Open "Connections" and choose "Propose a connection".
2. Pick the other listing, the type (collaborated, EU partner, member of, supplier, mentor), and the year or project.
3. The other owner gets one email and a notice in their account. They confirm or reject. Until they confirm, only the two of you and the admins see it.
4. You can withdraw a proposal at any time. A proposal nobody answers is withdrawn after 60 days. If it is rejected, you cannot propose the same type again for 30 days.
5. If the other listing is not claimed yet, the proposal waits. They see it when they claim.

Partner not listed here? Add an outside partner with a name and website. You must give a link that proves the connection, such as a project page. Outside partners are never published. The connection shows on your page only, marked "unverified".

You can send up to 10 proposals a day.

## Messages from visitors

Each listing has a contact form. Visitors write to us and we forward the message to your email. When you press reply, the answer goes straight to the visitor. We never store the message text. Each mail has a "Report" link for spam. If your email bounces, we mark it invalid and an admin will contact you. Please keep your contact email working.

## Views

"My listing" shows daily views of your page. We leave out bots, link previews and your own visits. We count visits without cookies.

## Confirm your listing is up to date

Every few months, press "Confirm my listing is up to date" in your account. The date shows on your page as "Last updated". It tells visitors the information is current. Admins can see listings that were not confirmed for 12 months.

## Download or delete your data

Open "My data".

- Download: you get a JSON file with your listing and account data.
- Hide: "Archive my listing" hides it. You can bring it back whenever you want.
- Delete: "Delete my listing and account" archives it at once and erases it after 14 days. Until then you can cancel. After that, your listing, images, translations, contact data and connections are gone. Records of changes lose your personal details.
- Deleting uses a button in your account, never a link in an email you did not ask for.

## People on the registry

The registry also lists individual innovators. A few extra rules protect them.

- Your listing is published only with your consent. It is a separate box that says exactly what we publish. We store the date and version of the text you agreed to. You must also confirm that you are 16 or older.
- The map shows your town, not your home. The pin sits near the town and is moved by a fixed random offset, so nobody can find your street. People never have an exact address.
- Your full name is part of the page address and can appear in Google and other search engines.
- You can add a photo. It is delivered through Cloudinary, which stores images in the United States under EU safeguards. [[GAP: lawyer to confirm wording of the safeguard statement]]
- You can withdraw consent at any time in your account. Your listing is unpublished at once.

## Something wrong with a listing?

Use "Report this listing" on its page. An admin looks at it. Nothing is hidden automatically.
