# About the DNŽ Innovators Registry

The Registry of Innovators of Dubrovnik-Neretva County is a public list of people and organizations that work on innovation in our region. It is run by the Association of Innovators of Dubrovnik-Neretva County. Its purpose is to help innovators find each other, to let schools, companies, public bodies and the media find them, and to show how many innovative people and ideas the county has.

The registry is not a state record, and being listed gives no legal status. [[GAP: confirm this sentence with the association.]]

## Who can be listed

- Individual innovators who live or work in Dubrovnik-Neretva County.
- Organizations in the county that work on innovation: associations, companies, crafts, institutions, schools and others.

## What is public

For organizations: name, description, what they offer and look for, website and a contact they choose. For people: name, town (never an exact address, and the map pin is moved on purpose within the town), description and anything else they choose to publish. Each person decides what is visible to everyone and what only to members. People give a separate consent and can withdraw it at any time, after which the listing is hidden at once. Personal listings can appear in search engines. [[GAP: a lawyer should check this paragraph and the privacy policy.]]

## How to apply

1. Open the Apply page. You do not need an account.
2. Fill in a name, a short description, one contact, a country and a town. Everything else is optional.
3. Confirm your email address with the link we send.
4. The association reviews your application within seven days. If something is missing, we tell you what, and you can fix it and send it again.
5. When we approve, you get a link to set a password. Then you can edit your listing, hide it, or delete it at any time.
