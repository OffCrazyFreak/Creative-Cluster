# About Creative Cluster

Creative Cluster is a map and a meeting place for cultural and creative organizations. It helps you find who works near you, who works on similar things in other countries, and who might join you in a new project.

It is part of Restorative Practices, a project co-funded by the European Union under the Creative Europe programme. Restorative Practices brings together organizations that work where sustainability and bio-art meet. It builds on transfeminist ways of working and supports collectives that have been left out too often. It wants to bring creative work to rural and remote places, and to help European art travel further.

## Who runs it

The project is led by A Casa do Río (Spain). The partners are Bioart Society (Finland), Cultivamos Cultura (Portugal) and UR Institute (Croatia). This website is run by Udruga inovatora Dubrovačko-neretvanske županije (Inovatori DNŽ), a Croatian association. ## Who can join

Cultural and creative organizations, collectives and projects can add a listing, in any country. Organizations in the Restorative Practices network are listed too. [[GAP: any limits, for example sectors, countries, non-profit only. The old project text names no conditions.]] Listings are checked by a person before they appear.

## How it works

- _The map and the list._ Every listing is on a map and in a list. Filter by topic, country or what an organization offers or looks for.
- _Opportunities._ Organizations can post what they are looking for or can offer, for example a partner, a skill, a space or a funding call partner. Posts close on their own after a while.
- _Connections._ Organizations can say who they have worked with. The other side confirms it, then the link shows on the map.
- _Language._ The site works in English and Croatian. Text written in one language is machine translated into the other, and marked as such.
