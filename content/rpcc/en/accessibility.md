# Accessibility statement

This statement covers {{siteName}} ({{siteAddress}}), run by Udruga inovatora Dubrovačko-neretvanske županije.

## This statement is voluntary

The law on web accessibility in Croatia (NN 17/19) and the EU Web Accessibility Directive apply to public sector bodies. We are an association, so they do not apply to us. We publish this statement anyway, because we want the site to work for everyone. We followed the structure of the EU model statement (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1523).

## Our aim

We build the site to meet WCAG 2.2, level AA. We have not had it checked by an outside auditor, so we do not claim full conformance. We say it is partly conformant: we aim for AA, and the known gaps are listed below.

## How we built for it

- The list and directory pages are the main way to use the site. Everything the map shows is also in the list, with the same filters.
- You can use the whole site with a keyboard. A "skip to content" link jumps past the map.
- The number of results is announced to screen readers when you change a filter.
- Buttons and links have large enough touch targets, and keyboard focus is never hidden behind other elements.
- Moving a pin or sorting by dragging always has a keyboard alternative, and you never have to drag to do something.
- Camera movements on the map are switched off if your device asks for reduced motion.

## Known limits

1. **The map.** The map is a drawn canvas. A screen reader cannot read it. Use the list or directory pages instead: they hold the same entries, the same filters and the same details, and they are the main interface.
2. **Machine translations.** Descriptions are translated between Croatian and English by a machine. They can be wrong or awkward. Translated text is marked "Machine translated" and the original is one click away. The language of translated text is set in the page code, so screen readers should pronounce it correctly.
3. **Images and files from owners.** Entry owners upload logos, photos and sometimes links to PDF files. Some images may have weak or missing descriptions, and some PDF files may not be accessible. We cannot fix other people's files, but we can ask the owner to improve them. Write to us and we will help.
4. **Map base layer.** The map tiles come from a free provider. Their colors and labels may not meet the contrast level of WCAG 2.2 AA. This does not affect the list.
5. **Possible other gaps.** We have not tested with every screen reader and browser.

## How we assessed this

This is a self-assessment by the people who built the site. We checked against WCAG 2.2 AA by hand during development. We have not yet run automated accessibility tests and have not hired an outside audit. We plan to add automated checks later. [[GAP: confirm the plan and the person responsible]]

## Tell us about a problem

If something does not work for you, or you need information in another form, write to {{contactEmail}}. Tell us the page, what you tried, and what device or assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within 5 working days and to tell you then what we will do. [[GAP: confirm 5 working days is realistic for the clients]]

If you are not happy with our answer about personal data, you can contact the Croatian data protection authority (AZOP, azop.hr). For accessibility itself there is no authority that oversees voluntary sites like ours.
