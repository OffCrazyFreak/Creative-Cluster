Last updated: [[GAP: go-live date]]

It is run by Udruga inovatora Dubrovačko-neretvanske županije (the association, we, us) on behalf of the project. By using the site or opening a listing you accept these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the site. Browsing is free and open to everyone.

## 1. Who may list

Organisations and people working in the cultural and creative fields with an interest in restorative practices may ask for a listing. An organisation listing must be submitted by someone allowed to speak for it. You apply without an account. We normally review within 7 days. We may decline an application and will give a reason. You can fix a declined application and send it again. Accounts are created only by invitation or after an application is approved.

## 2. Accuracy

You must enter correct, current and non-misleading information and fix it when it changes. The button "Confirm my listing is up to date" shows you checked it. We do not check every listing. Being listed is not an endorsement by the association, the project partners or the European Union, and does not prove anyone's identity, qualifications or results. Please do your own checks before working with anyone.

## 6. Contact form

You send messages to listing owners through our form. We do not show the owner's address. Your message and your email address (so the owner can reply) are passed on to the owner. Rules: the message must be personal and about the listing; at most 1,000 characters and one link; no advertising or chain messages. Limit: 3 messages per sender per hour. We do not send you a copy; you see a confirmation on screen. You can report abuse with the "Report" link in a received message. Owners do not have to reply.

## 7. Opportunities board

Only owners of published listings can post opportunities (for example open calls, residencies, offers to collaborate, jobs), up to 3 active per listing. The first post of each listing is reviewed; later posts go live at once (we can change this per listing). A post runs until its deadline plus 1 day, or 60 days if there is no deadline (180 days at most), and can be renewed up to 3 times. Posts must be real offers, must not ask applicants for money, and must not mislead or discriminate. Replies go only through the contact form. We, the project partners and the European Union are not a party to, or a broker of, any agreement that comes out of an opportunity.

## 8. Connections between listings

The owner of a listing can propose a connection (for example "works with" or "partner") to another listing. A connection becomes public only when both sides confirm. The other side can decline, and either side can withdraw. An unanswered proposal is withdrawn after 60 days. A declined connection of the same kind cannot be proposed again for 30 days. Only propose connections that really exist. If the other listing has no owner account yet, the proposal waits; an administrator may confirm on its behalf, and this is recorded. Links to outside partners without a listing are shown only on the proposer's page, marked unverified, with a source link as evidence.

## 9. Review, editing, reports and removal

- **Approve once, then trust.** After the first approval your edits go live at once and are logged. An administrator can send a listing's edits back through review.
- **What administrators may do.** They may correct or revert edits, suspend a listing, remove a listing or posts, and ban an account, if the content breaks these terms, the law or others' rights, is clearly wrong or out of date, or the site is being misused.
- **Reports.** Every listing has a "Report this listing" link. Nothing is hidden automatically. Administrators review reports and decide.
- **Reasons.** When we restrict or remove a listing or account, we email the owner what we did and why, unless the law forbids it. A suspended listing shows "410 gone" to visitors. If you disagree, reply to that email or write to the address in the imprint. We will look at the decision again and restore the listing if we got it wrong.
- **Unlawful content.** If someone tells us about content they believe is unlawful, we will review it without delay and remove it if it is.

## 10. Machine translation

You write your description in one language (English or Croatian). The other language version is made by a computer. It may contain mistakes and does not replace the original. Translated text is labelled "Machine translated" and you can view the original. The owner can edit the translation, which then counts as a human translation. If a translation fails our checks, we show the original with a notice.

## 11. Account, ending and deletion

Your account is personal; do not share your password. At any time you can archive your listing (hide it; it can be restored) or ask to delete your listing and account. Deletion happens after 14 days unless you cancel. We may ban an account or remove a listing if these terms are broken. Unclaimed listings that we imported ourselves from public sources are archived automatically 12 months after import; an administrator can restore them, and the owner can claim them at any time.
